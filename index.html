Settings

Key binds

Click a lane, then press the key you want for it. Defaults are A S K L.

Note speed

How fast notes travel toward the hit line. The preview on the right scrolls at the chosen speed.

Master volume

Volume of everything except the menu music: the songs you play and the song previews on the song list.

Bass

Turns the low end of all the music up or down: songs, song previews, menu music and the Game Over music. 0 dB is the original sound.

Menu music volume

Volume of the music on the main menu, in settings, on the pause screen, on the results screen and on the Game Over screen.

Reset progress

Puts this save back to how it started: coins, best scores, everything you've bought and all settings (codes stay used). Your other save isn't touched.

Audio offset

If your hits keep landing late, move the slider right. If they land early, move it left. The game shows EARLY or LATE under each hit that isn't perfect.

Auto offsetPress Start, then tap one of your lane keys (or click the box) on every tick. Four ticks count you in, then 16 are measured, and the offset is set for you.

Add a song

Pick an MP3 from your device. The game listens to it, finds the beat and the chorus, makes Easy, Medium, Hard and Extreme maps, and matches its volume to the other songs. Added songs are saved in this browser.

Want a song from YouTube? Convert the video to an MP3 at ytmp3.gl, then choose the file here. Only use videos you have the rights to.
